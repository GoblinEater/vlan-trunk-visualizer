VLAN Trunk Visualizer

Three switches, three VLANs, nine hosts distributed across the fabric. Each solid port nub is an access port, hard-assigned to one VLAN. Each striped nub is a trunk port — a single physical link carrying all three VLANs at once, kept apart only by their 802.1Q tag, never by separate wires.

What to watch: pick any host's color and follow it — every frame you see for that color stays inside its VLAN, even crossing two switches on the exact same trunk wire the other VLANs are using. Uncheck a VLAN to isolate the other two. Every so often a broadcast fans out to every host in one VLAN only, on every switch, simultaneously — and the other VLANs never see it.
Ports: solid nub = access port, wired to one VLAN only. Striped nub = trunk port, one physical link tagged for all three.
